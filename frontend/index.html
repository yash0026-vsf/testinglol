Autonomous Agent Capabilities

This portal demonstrates a complete Multi-Agent Customer Support system powered by LangGraph.

Intelligent Ticket Router

The system analyzes every incoming message for intent (billing, technical, etc.), sentiment, and urgency, dynamically routing to specialized AI sub-agents.

Knowledge Reasoning

A built-in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engine fetches official company policies (refunds, SLAs) to provide highly accurate, hallucination-free answers.

Complaint Investigation

If a user is highly upset, the AI switches to a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) mode. It asks clarifying diagnostic questions rather than just categorizing the complaint.

Analytics Engine

An asynchronous endpoint scans through historical conversation threads in the database to identify recurring issues, negative sentiment trends, and early churn signals.

Voice Interface (Speech)

Speak your requests out loud using the microphone button! The AI utilizes native browser Speech-to-Text and will speak its answers back to you dynamically.

Auto-Translation

Multilingual support is fully automated. Send a message in Spanish or French, and the AI will detect the language, process it, and respond in your native tongue!

Action & Tool Execution

The AI goes beyond giving text advice. Ask it to "Refund my order" or "Cancel my subscription" and watch it execute actual Python backend functions autonomously.

AI vs AI Simulation

A standalone Python script allows an "Angry Customer AI" to argue with our Support AI, proving the system can handle extreme conflict and human escalation perfectly.
